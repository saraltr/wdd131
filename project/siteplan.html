Gardenia Site Plan

Site Name: Gardenia

Gardenia is an interactive floral boutique website. The name is inspired by the gardenia flower and how it is often associated with elegance and beauty. Gardenia also represents creativity and personal expression through floral design.

Optional domain availability: gardenia-shop.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of Gardenia is to create an interactive flower shop experience that allows users create their own bouquets, learn about various flower types, and explore a catalogue of pre-designed bouquets for the occation of their choice.

Scenarios

  • What types of flowers are best for creating a bouquet for a specific occasion?
  • What is the meaning of certain flowers? When is it commonly used?
  • What bouquets are available, and what occasions are they best suited for?
  • Which flowers are best for certain seasons?
